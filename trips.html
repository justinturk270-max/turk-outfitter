FAQ

Before you book

What's included in the price?

Lodging or camp, meals, guides, horses, and gear. Licenses, tips, and travel are not included.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. We tailor every trip to your skill and fitness level, and we're happy to teach.

What's the cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 90 days out, or transferable to another season.
