About

Three generations in the saddle

A family business built on respect for the land, the animals, and the people who join us.

Our Story

Started with a string of horses

In 1998, Jack Turk left a desk job to guide his first hunting party out of a borrowed cabin. Today his kids run the operation, with the same principles: fair chase, honest advice, and leave the country better than you found it.

We hold every required permit, carry full liability coverage, and reinvest in habitat and conservation projects across the region.
